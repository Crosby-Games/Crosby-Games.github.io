Privacy policy

Channel Autopilot · last updated 4 October 2026

This policy covers Channel Autopilot (“the app”), a private tool its owner uses to publish videos to the Borderline Explained YouTube channel. The owner is the app's only user. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

With the channel owner's permission, given through Google sign-in, the app accesses:

  • the owner's channel ID and name, to confirm it is acting on the right channel;
  • the channel's own videos, to upload them, set thumbnails and update titles and descriptions;
  • the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports: views, watch time, impressions and click-through rate.

It does not access viewers' personal information, comments, subscriber lists, or any other channel's data.

How the information is used

The information is used only to publish the channel's videos and to measure how they perform, for example to replace a thumbnail that few people click.

Storage

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. Video records and the channel's daily statistics are kept in a private database that only the owner can access. Analytics data is refreshed regularly and deleted after it is no longer needed, and in any case no later than 30 days after collection unless refreshed from the API.

Sharing

Nothing the app collects is sold, shared with or shown to anyone else, and none of it is used for advertising.

Cookies

The app does not place cookies or similar technology on anyone's device. This website does not use cookies or tracking.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google security settings page. To have stored data deleted, email the address below; it will be deleted within 7 days.
