Channel Autopilot

The private publishing tool behind the Borderline Explained YouTube channel.

The channel

Borderline Explained is a YouTube channel of short educational videos: why a city is where it is, how a border changed over the centuries, and how two countries compare by the numbers. Every fact comes from open sources, such as Wikipedia, Wikidata and World Bank Open Data, and images are public domain or Creative Commons and credited in each video's description.

What Channel Autopilot does

Channel Autopilot is a private tool run by the channel's owner on their own computer. The tool uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel's videos, set their thumbnails and update their titles and descriptions, and the YouTube Analytics API to read the channel's own viewing statistics. It acts only on this one channel, and the owner is its only user.

Videos use AI-generated narration and, sometimes, AI illustrations. They are labelled as altered or synthetic content where YouTube's rules require it.
